Privacy Policy and Data Deletion

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Who we are

This WhatsApp bot ("the Service") replies automatically to messages that people send to its WhatsApp Business number. Contact: yossef200mohamed@gmail.com

2. What data we process

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and the text of the messages you send to the Service.
  • Technical data needed to operate the Service, such as message IDs and timestamps.

3. Why we process it

Only to receive your message, send an automatic reply, prevent duplicate or abusive messages, and honor opt-out requests. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

4. How we protect it

Phone numbers are stored in a pseudonymised (hashed) form. Message content is kept only for a short period (72 hours) and then deleted automatically. Access to the system is restricted and secrets are never stored in the source code.

5. Sharing

Messages are transmitted through the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta. We do not share your data with any other third party, except where required by law.

6. Opt-out

Send "STOP" to the Service at any time and we will stop messaging you.

7. Data deletion instructions

To request deletion of your data, email yossef200mohamed@gmail.com from any address, include the phone number you used on WhatsApp, and we will delete the data associated with it within 30 days and confirm by email.

8. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
